📖 How to Play — Card Game

▼
The Deck (50 cards)

Two copies each of 0–20 (positive cards) · Eight negative cards: −10, −20, −30, −40, −50, −60, −70, −80 · Deck mean ≈ 1.2

Setup

6 cards go face-down as the community. Each player is dealt 1 private card from the same deck — giving you a clue about what's not in the community.

Objective

The settlement value is the sum of all 6 community cards. Buy low, sell high around your fair value estimate.

Fair Value

Use your private card(s) + any revealed community cards to narrow down the remaining unknowns. Your FV = revealed sum + (hidden count × expected value of remaining cards).

e.g. You hold +15. Community shows +3, +7 (2 revealed). You know those 3 cards aren't in the remaining 4 hidden slots → adjust EV upward.
Market Making

Each turn a player posts Bid / Ask. Everyone else races to trade. The market maker takes the other side of any trade.

Market 38 / 44 · Your FV = 48 → BUY at 44, edge = +4. Your FV = 32 → SELL at 38, edge = +6.
Turn Flow

Someone posts a market → you race to trade → if you trade, you become the new market maker → someone else trades with you → they become maker → and so on. If no one trades within the window, the maker role rotates automatically to the next player.

Three Rounds

After each round: 2 community cards are revealed and each player gets a new private card. Re-estimate FV each round.

Settlement

All 6 community cards are revealed. P&L = (Settlement − Avg Entry) × Position.

📖 How to Play — Estimathon

▼
Objective

The settlement value is the true answer to a real-world estimation question. Trade profitably around it.

Preview (90 seconds)

Before each round you get 90 seconds to form your own estimate. No hints in round 1.

Market Making

Each turn, a player posts a Bid / Ask. If you think the true answer is above the ask — buy. If below the bid — sell.

Q: "How many Starbucks locations worldwide?" — If your estimate is 36,000 and the market is 30k/34k, BUY has edge.
Turn Flow

Someone posts a market → you race to trade → if you trade, you become the new market maker → someone else trades with you → they become maker → and so on. If no one trades within the window, the maker role rotates to the next player.

Hints

A new hint unlocks each round, narrowing the true range. Adjust your estimate and your aggression accordingly.

Settlement

After round 3, the true answer is revealed. P&L = (True Answer − AvgEntry) × Position.
